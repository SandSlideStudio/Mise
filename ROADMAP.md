# Roadmap

What's planned after 2.2, roughly in order. Nothing here has a date.

- **Labels.** Print prep date, use-by and allergens to a label printer when a line is ticked.
- **Par levels.** Record what's already made, so the prep list only asks for the rest.
- **Temperature and cooling logs.** HACCP records kept on the tablet instead of on paper. Waiting
  on more research into the exact Dutch rules before it gets a version.
- **Costing.** Ingredient prices and purchase units, and a food cost per dish and per menu. Also
  waiting on research before it gets a version.

Not planned: shared recipe libraries across several restaurants. That only makes sense once a
second site exists.

Ideas and requests are welcome as issues.
